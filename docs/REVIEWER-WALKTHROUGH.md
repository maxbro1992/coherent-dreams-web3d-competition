# Reviewer walkthrough

This route demonstrates the complete project in about two minutes.

1. Open the prototype in a current Chromium browser. Confirm the renderer chip says **WebGPU active**; an automatic **WebGL 2 fallback** is valid on other hardware.
2. Drag the terrain to orbit, scroll to zoom, and adjust **Relief**. Geometry remains tied to the same scalar field.
3. Open **Source**. Compare the unmodified Cahn–Hilliard field with the human-directed artwork and read the provenance note.
4. Select **AI clusters**, then set **Scout objective** to **Find rare morphology**. The colors expose the four learned regimes and the finding card cites gradient, curvature, and rarity.
5. Press **N** twice. The marker and evidence change to spatially separated recommendations.
6. Select **Interfaces**, choose **Find active interfaces**, then click **Focus camera**. The camera moves to the chosen evidence patch.
7. Open **Method**. Review the local feature pipeline, deterministic clustering, grounded scout, privacy behavior, and scientific limits.
8. Press **1**, **2**, and **3** to verify keyboard view switching. Press **Space** to pause motion and **R** to reset the camera.

Expected baseline for the bundled field: 44/56% phase balance, 33.4% interface density, approximately 80 nm domain scale, 0.04 anisotropy, 89% normalized entropy, 110 connected islands, and approximately 68% aggregate cluster-assignment certainty. Timing varies by device.
