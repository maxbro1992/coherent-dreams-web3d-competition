# Official competition requirements checked

Checked against the official [AI & Web3D Innovation Competition 2026 page](https://web3d.siggraph.org/2026/ai-web3d-innovation-competition/) on 15 September 2026.

## Eligibility and deadline

- Open internationally to individuals and teams.
- Students, professionals, researchers, hobbyists, and other Web3D creators are invited.
- Team size may be up to five people; this entry is currently prepared for one entrant.
- Submission deadline: **30 September 2026**.
- Winners scheduled to be announced: **15 October 2026**.

## Required deliverables

- Working browser application accessible by URL.
- GitHub source repository, public or private with judge access.
- Technical documentation.
- Three-to-five-minute demonstration video.
- Innovation statement of at most 500 words.

The local package contains every deliverable except the three external URLs. The prepared innovation statement is 334 words. The rendered video is 4:50.021.

## Judging weights

- Innovation and creativity: 30%
- Technical implementation: 25%
- AI integration: 20%
- User experience and accessibility: 15%
- Impact and scalability: 10%

The project’s mapping to those criteria is documented in `JUDGING-MATRIX.md`.

## Prizes and rights note

- First prize: **USD 1,000**.
- Second prize: **USD 500**.

The public page does not state a transfer or licence of entrant intellectual property, and it allows a private repository when judges receive access. The public page does not list an entry fee.
